Help centre

How can we help?

Plain answers, no jargon. If an article does not solve it, chat opens with the context already attached, so you never repeat yourself.

How do I get a statement or tax certificate?
Go to My loan and tap Download statement. Pick any period and the PDF is generated on the spot. Tax certificates for the previous tax year appear there each July.
How is my interest rate decided?
We assess every person individually rather than applying one rate to everyone. On average our rates match the major banks and are sometimes lower, depending on your circumstances. Your rate is fixed in your agreement and shown on every statement.
My debit order bounced or I cannot pay this month. What now?
First: do not panic, and do not go quiet. Tap "I cannot make payment this month" in the menu on the left. A trained person, never a bot, will look at your circumstances and agree a workable arrangement with you, usually within two business hours. Telling us early always works in your favour, and it never counts against you.
Can I pay my loan off early?
Yes, any time, with no penalty. Tap Settlement quote for an instant figure that shows exactly how much interest you save. The quote is valid for 7 days and the settlement letter downloads immediately.
Can I get funding for my next semester or year?
Yes. Tap Request a top-up and we reassess your circumstances individually, the same way as your first application. Top-ups cover tuition, books and materials.
How long does my application take?
The My application page shows your live status. Individual assessment typically takes 2 to 3 working days once your documents are verified. If we need anything from you it appears there first, with a notification.
